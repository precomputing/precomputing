      Pulling the plug

      The Engine is thrown away in the middle of writing a checkpoint: the whole program and everything in its memory. SQLite rolls the half-written checkpoint back, a fresh copy of the Engine opens the file, and the feed resends every trade after the last one the file holds. At 13:15 the demo does this once by itself.

      The race: the Engine against the compiled SQL

      The first 200,000 trades of the day, rounded up to a whole second, through a fresh Engine and through the same policy compiled to SQLite triggers. Both take turns on the same thread in equal slices of time, then every value in the two files is compared.
