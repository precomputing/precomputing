    Gateways

    Each gateway serves its customers and reports every request to the meter. A client may retry through either one.

    GatewayLink to the meterDeliveredWaitingRetries

    Delivered and waiting count reports to the meter. A client that gets no answer in time sends the same request again, with the same request id, and the retry may land on the other gateway. eu serves Juniper, Northgate and Harbor; us serves Quarry, Maple and Sundial.

    The policy

    A short policy says what to count and when a month closes. The compiler turns it into the SQL this page runs; the billing tables sit next to it in the same file.

    
    
    
      
    Change the policy and compile it with the real compiler, built for the browser (about 1.3 MB to download). The running demo keeps its own policy.
